    How the scrambles work

    Last layer scrambles chain a random edge case, corner case and PLL from the 3x3 sections you have on, so you recognize and solve each step in order. F2L scrambles hide one pair under a messy top layer. Reveal shows the cases the generator used; if your algs differ, the middle steps can too.
